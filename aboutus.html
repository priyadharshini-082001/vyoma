ABOUT VYOMA NEXGEN ACADEMY

Learning that builds confidence, wellness and creativity.

A positive neighbourhood learning space where academics, wellness and creative expression come together.

Melbatchpet, Harur Established 2026

A place to learn, grow and express.

Vyoma NexGen Academy was established in 2026 with a vision to create a positive learning space where academics, wellness and creativity come together. Located at Melbatchpet, Harur, the academy currently offers three core programs — Tuition, Yoga and Dance. We understand that every learner has different abilities, interests and areas where support may be required. Our aim is to provide an encouraging atmosphere where participants can learn progressively and develop confidence through regular practice. Vyoma NexGen Academy seeks to become a trusted neighbourhood learning and activity centre where learning is meaningful, participation is enjoyable and personal development is encouraged.
