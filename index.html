About Us

Safe, Fun & Educational – Just What Your Child Needs

Welcome to Vyoma NexGen Academy, a learning and activity centre established in 2026 at Melbatchpet, Harur. The academy brings Tuition, Yoga and Dance together under one roof, providing children, students and individuals with opportunities for academic learning, physical wellness and creative development. At Vyoma NexGen Academy, we believe that development should go beyond academics. Good education builds knowledge, physical activity develops discipline and well-being, while creative activities encourage confidence and self-expression.

WHY CHOOSE VYOMA NEXGEN ACADEMY?

Where Every Little Step
Leads to Big Discoveries

Vyoma NexGen Academy combines three important areas of personal development within one learning environment. Our tuition sessions are designed to provide additional academic guidance and encourage better understanding and regular study habits. Morning yoga sessions encourage participants to develop a healthy and disciplined start to their day. Dance provides learners with an opportunity to develop rhythm, coordination, confidence and creative expression. Multiple tuition and yoga batches make it easier to select a suitable schedule.
