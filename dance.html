WEEKEND Dance Sessions

Dance brings together movement, rhythm, coordination and creativity. The Dance Program at Vyoma NexGen Academy provides learners with an engaging weekend activity where they can develop their movement skills while enjoying the learning process. The sessions aim to encourage confidence, coordination, rhythm and creative expression in a positive atmosphere.
